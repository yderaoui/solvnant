# PumpKart promotional posters

Purpose: two shareable campaign pictures requested by the owner, separate from game UI.

- Full grid: all 15 catalog racers with their exact current vehicles and readable names, presented in a five-column, three-row neon Tokyo roster poster.
- The Big Three: Axiom, Fomo and Pump race neck-and-neck across a checkered city finish line, with electric blue, orange/purple and lime green trails.
- Style: polished detailed racing-game renders; dark plum/black, hot pink #FF006E, cyan, glossy materials and restrained light bloom.
- Format: landscape approximately 3:2, opaque scene background; preserve native generated resolution in PNG masters and offer JPEG copies for sharing.
- Copy: PumpKart branding and pumpkart.racing. No launch date, token address, prices, invented sponsors or investment claims.
- Inputs: existing generated garage portraits and the official PumpKart wordmark. Preserve each racer identity, clothing, silhouette, vehicle and distinctive props.
- Acceptance: visually count all 15 distinct racers; check all three new mascots; inspect spelling, legibility, composition and frame edges.
- Provenance: built-in image_gen, using project artwork as references. Exact prompts and outputs are recorded alongside the finished images.

Solo finish-line series:

- Ansem: "UNLEASH THE BULL"; yellow-shirt rider charging over the checkered line on his black bull and monster-truck wheels.
- FaZe Banks: "BUILT DIFFERENT."; winning slide in the red/yellow/blue brick-built kart, preserving the cap, beard and hoodie.
- Rasmr: "FASTEST CHECKOUT."; chrome shopping-cart sprint with a playful victory toast.
- Keep a visible black-and-white finish line, the same neon Tokyo street palette, the official logo and pumpkart.racing on every poster.
