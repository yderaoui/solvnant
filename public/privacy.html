Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

TrackLab 3D ("we", "the game") is a free browser racing game at solvnant.vercel.app. This page explains what we collect and why. We keep it to what the game needs.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with X: your X user ID, username, display name and profile picture. During sign-in we also read your account's creation date and follower count to keep bots out (new or empty accounts can't race). We do not post, follow, read your messages or ask for write access.
  • If you play as a guest: the nickname you choose.
  • Game activity: your points balance and history (entry fees, prizes, bets, bonuses), the races you enter and your inputs during a race (throttle, steering, brake), which are saved so races can be replayed.
  • If you link a Solana wallet: your public wallet address and how much of the game's token it holds. You prove ownership by signing a free message; we never get access to your funds and never move anything.
  • If you submit an AI agent: its name and code. Agents that race may be shown publicly, including their code.
  • Technical data: a random ID stored in your browser and a login token, so the game remembers you. Our hosting providers keep standard request logs.

What is public

Your username or nickname, race results, leaderboards, saved race replays and submitted agents are visible to everyone. Your points history, bets and wallet address are not shown to other players.

How we use it

Only to run the game: sign you in, keep bots out, run races, keep score, settle bets and show leaderboards and replays. We do not sell your data, show ads or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

The game runs on Cloudflare (game server and accounts), Supabase (race data) and Vercel (website). Wallet balances are looked up through a Solana RPC provider and token prices through DexScreener.

Points

Points are play money. They have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from the account menu, and unlink a wallet there.
  • You can revoke the game's access to your X account in X's settings under "Connected apps".
  • To have your account and data deleted, contact us (below). Race replays you appeared in may keep your name.

Children

The game is not meant for children under 13, and X accounts require users to be at least 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update the date above. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/yderaoui/solvnant.
